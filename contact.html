What to include when you reach out

The faster Luke can book your trip, the more time he can spend planning it. Send him:

  • Dates — one day, a few days, or a window of "anywhere in this range"
  • Group size — 1, 2, or a larger party
  • Experience level — first-time, occasional, or serious angler
  • Trip type — half-day, full-day wade, full-day float, multi-day
  • Anything else — lodging help, special occasions, gear questions
