Quality gear sized for you. If you'd rather fish your own, bring it.

From a meeting point in Aspen to the water and back.

Colorado fishing license required — Luke will walk you through it. ~$X for a day, ~$X for a year, depending on residency.

Layers (Aspen mornings are cold even in July). Polarized sunglasses. Sunscreen. A hat. Lunch preferences if you have any. Anything you'd like Luke to know about your experience level.
