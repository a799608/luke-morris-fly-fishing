Why fish with Luke

A guide with a real lineage.

Aspen has plenty of guides. Few of them came up where Luke did.

Penn State alum

Statistics major, economics minor. He speaks the language of the people he guides — and he loved every football Saturday in Beaver Stadium.

Trained on the Godfather's water

Four years fishing Spring Creek, Penns Creek, Spruce Creek — the Central PA limestone streams where Joe Humphreys, the Godfather of American fly fishing, has fished and taught for nine decades.

Eastern roots, western water

Grew up on the Lehigh, Mud Run, and the Pocono tributaries. Fished the Beaverkill and the upper Delaware. Now applying every lesson to the Roaring Fork Valley.

First time on the water? Even better.

Luke spent his Penn State years volunteering with a program that taught disabled veterans with PTSD how to fly fish. He has seen what the right kind of patience can do. If you have never picked up a fly rod, he is exactly the guide you want.
